## What survives from this historical metaphor

The admitted original RAW/CFA bytes and capture metadata must remain preserved as the measurement record. They may not be silently rewritten and then presented as the original camera evidence.

## What is superseded

The word **sealed** must not be interpreted to mean that TruthRaw reconstruction is enclosed by the source RAW, its bit depth, pixel lattice, WhiteLevel, ISO scale, camera colour basis, DNG representation, `[0,1]` range or float32 precision.

TruthRaw's current vision is a **Free Scientific Space** reconstructed from preserved evidence.

The Scientific Master may use a richer numerical/spatial/colour representation than the source container, while retaining explicit measured/reconstructed/censored/unknown/counterfactual/appearance authority.

Permanent rule:

> **Representation can exceed the source. Knowledge claims cannot exceed the evidence.**

> **Measured where measured. Reconstructed where necessary. Never invented.**

For future chats, do not bootstrap the project from the old sealed-house wording. Read the Free Scientific Space documents above instead.
