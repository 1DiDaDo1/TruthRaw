# D.RAW Open-World Evolution Law v0.1

Status: **PROJECT LAW CANDIDATE**

D.RAW must never confuse immutable evidence with immutable interpretation.

## Core law

> **Seal the evidence, not the thinking.**

A sealed source hash says which bytes were observed.
It does not say that today's reconstruction model, calibration model, optical
model, relation taxonomy or interpretation is the final possible model.

## Consequences

1. Historical evidence objects remain immutable.
2. Historical validation results remain reproducible.
3. Scientific interpretation may gain new versioned successors.
4. A successor may add information without rewriting its parent evidence.
5. UNKNOWN is not failure; it is an explicit open place for future knowledge.
6. New model families must not require mutation of old source evidence.
7. A current schema is a minimum semantic contract, not a claim that no other
   scientific concept can exist.
8. Closed enumerations should be used only where closure is itself part of the
   evidence law. Otherwise prefer versioned extension points.
9. Relations may be revised or superseded by stronger evidence, but historical
   certificates are preserved as provenance.
10. Representation freedom never upgrades evidence authority.

This law applies to new D.RAW work unless a narrower immutable contract is
scientifically necessary and explicitly justified.
